How the counts are made

  • Words: pieces of text separated by spaces or line breaks. Urdu and English words are counted separately.
  • Characters: every character, with and without spaces.
  • Sentences: ends marked by a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark, including Urdu ۔ and ؟.
  • Paragraphs: blocks of text separated by an empty line.
  • Read time uses the reading speed you choose. Speaking time uses 130 words per minute.

These are estimates. Reading and speaking times vary from person to person.
