What is Zakat?

Zakat is one of the five pillars of Islam: a yearly obligation to give a share of wealth to those in need. The standard rate on qualifying wealth is 2.5%.

How this calculator works

  • Net wealth = total zakatable wealth minus debts due now.
  • Nisab is the minimum threshold. This calculator uses 612.36 g of silver or 87.48 g of gold, multiplied by today's price per gram that you enter.
  • If net wealth is below nisab, Zakat is not due. If it is at or above nisab, the result is 2.5% of net wealth.

Common questions

Which nisab should I use? Many scholars and institutions use silver because its threshold is lower, so more people are included. Some use gold. If you are unsure, ask a local scholar or your mosque.

Does the wealth need to be held for a full year? Traditionally, yes, for most wealth in the lunar (Hijri) year. Check with a scholar for your situation.

Is this a religious ruling? No. This is a simple estimate. Eligible assets, liabilities, and local rules can change the final amount. Consult a qualified scholar for your circumstances.
