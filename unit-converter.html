Unit Converter Online (Free)

Convert between metric, imperial, South Asian and digital units. Pick a category, choose the units, type a value and press Convert.

Unit converter

1 seer = 80 tola (about 933.1 g). 1 maund = 40 seer (about 37.32 kg).

How to use this calculator

  1. Choose a category (for example Length or Weight).
  2. Type the value you want to convert.
  3. Choose the unit you have (From) and the unit you want (To).
  4. Press Convert. Use Swap units to reverse the direction, or Reset to start again.

Supported categories and units

  • Length: millimetre, centimetre, metre, kilometre, inch, foot, yard, mile
  • Weight: gram, kilogram, tola, seer, maund, pound, ounce
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin
  • Speed: km/h, m/s, mph, knot, ft/s
  • Volume: litre, millilitre, cubic metre, US gallon, US quart, US cup, US fluid ounce, US tablespoon, US teaspoon
  • Time: millisecond, second, minute, hour, day, week, month (average), year (365.2425 days)
  • Data: bit, byte, KB, MB, GB, TB (decimal) and KiB, MiB, GiB, TiB (binary)
  • Area: square centimetre, square metre, square foot, square yard, acre, hectare, square kilometre, square mile
  • Numeral system: decimal, binary, octal, hexadecimal (whole numbers only)

Examples

  • 1 mile = 1.609 km
  • 100 °F = 37.78 °C
  • 1 tola = 11.6638 g (1 seer = 80 tola)
  • 1 GB = 1,000 MB (decimal); 1 GiB = 1,024 MiB (binary)
  • 255 (decimal) = FF (hexadecimal) = 11111111 (binary)

Formulas and rules

  • Other units: value × factor of the From unit ÷ factor of the To unit, using one base unit per category.
  • Temperature: Fahrenheit to Celsius = (F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9; Kelvin = Celsius + 273.15. Below −273.15 °C is rejected.
  • Negative values are accepted for temperature only. For other categories, negative values are rejected.
  • Numeral system: whole numbers only. Values are converted using their base.

Frequently asked questions

Why are tola and seer values fixed? The page uses the standard conversions shown on it: 1 tola = 11.6638 g, 1 seer = 80 tola, 1 maund = 40 seer. Local trade practice can differ, so check with your seller for exact weights.

Can I convert negative numbers? Only for temperature. Length, weight and the other categories need zero or positive values.

Is my data saved? No. Values are used only in your browser and are not stored or sent anywhere.
