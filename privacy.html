Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Calculations

All calculators on Calculator10 run in your browser. The numbers you enter are calculated on your device. We do not send them to any server, and we do not store them.

The calculation history on the calculators stays in your browser's memory only. It disappears when you refresh or close the page.

2. Colour skin and saved settings

If you choose a colour skin (Classic, Golden, Dark or Pink), the choice is saved in your own browser so it is remembered next time. It is never sent anywhere.

3. Offline use

To work offline, the site saves its own files (pages, icons and fonts) in your browser's cache. You can remove them by clearing the site data in your browser settings.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: the site uses Google Fonts for its text style. When the font loads, your IP address and browser details are sent to Google, as with any web font.
  • Optional translation: in the Formulas section, some languages use Google Translate. This happens only after you confirm. Only the English formula text is sent, never your numbers.
  • Voice input (optional): the Speak button uses your browser's own speech recognition, which may send your voice to its provider (for example Google in Chrome or Apple in Safari). It starts only after you tap Speak and confirm, and your browser asks for microphone permission.
  • Share buttons: WhatsApp, Facebook, X, Telegram and the result share buttons are plain links. They open those services in a new tab only when you tap them, and share only the text or link you chose.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's own privacy policy.

5. Advertising

We may show advertisements on this site to support its free use. If advertising is active, the ads will be provided by a third-party advertising network. This section describes how that works:

  • The ad network may use cookies or similar technologies to limit how often you see an ad, to measure ad performance, and to show ads that may be relevant to you.
  • Ads are placed on the page only. Your calculation inputs and results are never shared with the ad network.
  • Where local law requires it, you will be asked for your consent before non-essential cookies are set.
  • You can control cookies in your browser settings, including blocking third-party cookies. Your browser's settings also let you clear existing cookies.
  • Ad network privacy policies apply to what they collect. Please read the policy linked from any advertisement you click.

6. Children

This site is for general use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page, with a new "Last updated" date.
