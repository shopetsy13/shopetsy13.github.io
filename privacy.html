Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Calculations

All calculators on Calculator10 run in your web browser on your own device. The numbers and text you enter are used only to produce the result. They are not sent to our servers and we do not store them.

The calculation history shown on some calculators stays in your browser for the current visit only. It disappears when you refresh or close the page.

2. Settings saved on your device

Two small settings are saved in your browser's local storage so that they are remembered next time:

  • your chosen colour skin (Classic, Golden, Dark or Pink), and
  • whether the on-screen celebration effects are on or off.

These settings never leave your device. You can remove them by clearing the site data in your browser settings.

3. Offline use

To let the calculators work without internet, the site saves its own files (pages, scripts, icons and the fonts it uses) in your browser's cache after your first visit. You can remove these files by clearing the site data in your browser settings.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

  • Cookies: Calculator10 does not set cookies of its own.
  • Analytics: we do not use analytics or tracking tools, such as Google Analytics, on this site.
  • Advertising: at the moment this site does not show advertisements, and it does not use advertising cookies.
  • Future advertising: we may apply to show ads through Google AdSense in future. If we do, this policy will be updated before any ads appear. It will then explain which cookies are used to show and measure ads, how you can manage them, and it will link to Google's advertising settings. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent first.

5. Third-party services

  • Hosting: the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub may process standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. The domain name is managed through Namecheap.
  • Google Fonts: the site loads its text fonts from Google Fonts. When a font loads, your IP address and browser details are sent to Google, as with any web font.
  • Optional translation: in the Formulas section, some languages use Google Translate. This happens only after you confirm. Only the English formula text is sent, never your numbers.
  • Voice input (optional): the Speak button uses your browser's own speech recognition, which may send your voice to its provider (for example Google in Chrome or Apple in Safari). It starts only after you tap Speak and confirm, and your browser asks for microphone permission.
  • Share buttons: WhatsApp, Facebook, X, Telegram and the result share buttons are plain links. They open those services in a new tab only when you tap them, and share only the text or link you chose.

6. Children

This site is for general use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your choices

Because we do not collect or store your calculation data, there is nothing to request or delete on our side. You can clear the site data in your browser settings at any time, and you can stop the browser from asking for microphone access.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page, with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, email shopetsy13@gmail.com, or visit the Contact Us page.
