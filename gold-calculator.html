Gold value calculator

1 tola = 11.6638 g, 1 tola = 12 masha, 1 masha = 8 ratti. Enter the current market rate yourself; jewellery making charges and wastage are not included.

How to use this calculator

  1. Type the weight of the gold.
  2. Choose the unit: tola, gram, masha or ratti.
  3. Choose the karat (24K, 22K, 21K or 18K).
  4. Type the price per tola and press Calculate gold value.

Examples

  • 1 tola at 200,000 per tola, 22K: about 183,333
  • 10 grams, 24K, at 200,000 per tola: about 17,166
  • 1 masha, 21K, at 200,000 per tola: about 17,500

Formulas and rules

  • 1 tola = 11.6638 grams. 1 tola = 12 masha. 1 masha = 8 ratti.
  • Gold value = weight in tola × price per tola × karat ÷ 24.
  • The result is an estimate. Dealer buying and selling rates can differ.

Frequently asked questions

What is a tola? A tola is a traditional unit of weight used for gold in South Asia. One tola is about 11.66 grams.

What does 22K mean? 22K means 22 parts gold out of 24, which is about 91.7% pure. The calculator uses karat ÷ 24.

Is this the price I will get at a shop? No. Shops add making charges and may pay a different buying rate. Ask your jeweller for the current rate.
