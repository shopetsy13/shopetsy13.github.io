About Calculator10

Calculator10 is a free website with online calculators and reference tools for everyday maths, money, health, study and land measurement. You do not need to create an account or install an app to use it.

Why this website exists

Many calculators are cluttered with adverts or ask for personal details before they give an answer. Calculator10 is built to be simple: type your numbers, see the result, and move on. The site is designed to work well on a phone, which is how many people use it.

What you can find here

  • Everyday: simple and scientific calculators, percentage, discount, age, land area (marla, kanal, acre, sq ft and more) and a unit converter covering length, weight, temperature, speed, volume, time, data, area and number systems.
  • Study: a formulas library with 35 common maths formulas, explained in 24 languages.
  • Money: loan EMI, Zakat, GST and tax, and gold value calculators.
  • Health: BMI, daily calorie estimate and water intake estimate.
  • Writing: a word and character counter for English and Urdu text.
  • Fun: a simple name-based compatibility calculator.

Some calculators also have their own page with a full guide, including the formulas used, examples and frequently asked questions.

How the calculations work

The calculations run in your web browser on your own device. The numbers you type are not sent to our servers, and we do not store them. Your calculation history stays in the browser for the current visit only.

After your first visit, the site can save its own files in your browser so that the calculators still open without an internet connection. Some optional features, such as voice input and translation of formula text, use services provided by your browser or by Google, and they start only when you choose them. The Privacy Policy explains these in detail.

Important limits

  • Results are estimates for general use. They are not financial, legal, religious or medical advice.
  • Gold prices, land measurement sizes and nisab values can differ by place and date. Check current local figures and official documents where it matters.
  • For health, pregnancy, medicines or a weight plan, speak to a doctor or registered dietitian.
